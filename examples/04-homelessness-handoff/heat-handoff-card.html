The handoff

Two cards from one set of confirmed facts. Neither one can say anything the call did not establish.

Locked until the call is confirmed

Still needed:

    Do not send anyone yet

    1. Try the next listed cooling center in the city directory and call that one.
    2. If no listed center confirms it is open, call 211 (United Way of Central Alabama) and ask for an open cooling location today.
    3. If the need is shelter rather than heat relief, route through One Roof Coordinated Entry. This card does not make placement decisions.
    4. Stay with the immediate need in the meantime: shade and water do not require a building.

    Sending someone to walk to a closed building during a heat advisory is the specific harm this step exists to prevent. That is why no handoff card is produced here.

    Why it is built this way

    • The gate is the point. HUD PIT/HIC counts are historical and system-level; no Birmingham source publishes live cooling-center capacity. A page that displayed a resource list would be quietly claiming something it cannot know.
    • Two cards, one set of facts. The person-facing card can only say what the call confirmed. Unconfirmed items become "we could not check", never silence.
    • Freshness expires. The card ages visibly and marks itself expired at the 2-hour follow-up window, so a stale card is not quietly reused on a later shift.
    • No scoring. Nothing here ranks, triages, or rates a person.

    Limits

    • SCEN-01 is fictional. The demo values name a fictional center. Real use means typing in a real listing from the city directory or 211.
    • Nothing is stored. No localStorage, no server, no record. Closing the tab discards everything, deliberately.
    • This is heat relief only. Shelter, housing, and placement run through One Roof Coordinated Entry.
    • Built as a worked example of the 30-minute quick start in resources/04-homelessness-handoff.md.
