Heat Handoff Card

Birmingham lists eleven real cooling stations, but they open only when the heat index hits 95°F — and the city publishes no phone number for any of them. So this card refuses to produce a handoff until a person has confirmed a station is actually open today. The phone call is the artifact. Everything below it is just the confirmed facts, written out twice — once for the responder's log, once for the person walking there.

    Why it is built this way

    • A list is not a schedule. The city publishes eleven stations, but they activate only at a 95°F heat index. Showing the list alone would imply eleven open doors.
    • One number, and it is correct. The city publishes no phone number for any station. Rather than source eleven numbers from eleven other sites — any of which could be wrong — everything routes through 211, which is free and stable.
    • 211 is a referral line, not a system of record. Its own heat guidance lists no Birmingham addresses and never uses the city's term "cooling station". The card says so rather than implying a data link that does not exist.
    • Eligibility travels with the address. Pathways serves women and children only. That restriction appears in the dropdown, on the responder's log, and as the first line of the person's card — before anything about hours.
    • Two cards, one set of facts. The person-facing card can only say what the call confirmed. Unconfirmed items become "we could not check", never silence.
    • Freshness expires. The card ages visibly and marks itself expired at the 2-hour follow-up window, so a stale card is not quietly reused on a later shift.
    • No scoring. Nothing here ranks, triages, or rates a person.

    Limits

    • The station list is a snapshot taken 28 August 2026, not a feed. Check birminghamal.gov/homelessness before relying on it.
    • The person is fictional — SCEN-01 from the event's synthetic dataset. The stations are real. Keep those straight when showing this to anyone.
    • No overnight option exists. Every listed station closes by 8 p.m. at the latest, most by 5 or 6. Sunday coverage is thinnest — only Harrison Rec and Firehouse publish Sunday hours.
    • Entry requirements are not published by the city, in either direction. The card never says "no ID needed" on its own authority — only a responder who asked can record that.
    • Nothing is stored. No localStorage, no server, no record. Closing the tab discards everything, deliberately.
    • Heat relief only. Shelter, housing, and placement run through One Roof Coordinated Entry — which is also, itself, one of the eleven listed stations.
