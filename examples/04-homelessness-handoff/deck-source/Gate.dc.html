What we built

A card that refuses to help too early

No handoff is produced until a person has called and written down what they were told. Everything after that point is only the confirmed facts.

The output stays visible but locked, and names exactly what is still missing. Hiding it would just look broken.

Locked until the call is confirmed Still needed:
—which station 211 named
—whether anyone confirmed it is open today
—what time it is open until
—the time you called 211
—your initials, so the follow-up has an owner

The phone call is the part that cannot be automated, so the card is built around protecting it.
