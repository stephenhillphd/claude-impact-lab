Considerations & Limitations

It goes stale

A snapshot from one day. Nothing here tells you when it stopped being true.

Nights are uncovered

The latest close is 8 p.m., and that is two libraries on Tuesdays. Ten of eleven shut by 6, one at 12:30. Sundays, only two open at all.

Entry rules are unknown

No source states an ID or residency rule, and none rules one out. The card never claims “no ID needed” on its own authority.

Getting there is unsolved

Nothing we found says how a person reaches a station. A confirmed address two miles away, on foot, at 99°F, is not a plan.

It needs people

The card only works because a person makes the call. So the limit is how many volunteers are out during an advisory.

Marcus is invented, and so is the hour, the temperature, the street and the volunteer. The eleven stations and their hours are real, cited on slide 3.
