The near miss

Station 03 would not have taken him

Station 03 on that list Pathways 409 Richard Arrington Jr. Blvd. North City-published hours: Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–3 p.m. Serves women and children only

The city's list says nothing about eligibility for any of the eleven. We only found this on the organization's own site.

On the list it looks like the other ten: a name, an address, opening hours. Nothing on it tells a volunteer that this one cannot take a man in his fifties — and he is the one who would have made the walk in the heat.

So eligibility rides with the address: in the dropdown, on the responder's log, and as the first line of the card handed over, ahead of anything about hours.

Known for one station out of eleven. The other ten may have restrictions nobody has published.  ·  Marcus is fictional; Pathways and its restriction are real.
